SkillingFree is an independent learning platform designed to help people explore technology, develop practical skills, and navigate their learning journey with greater clarity.

Microsoft AI Skill Tree

The Microsoft AI Skill Tree is an interactive learning roadmap within SkillingFree. It organizes Microsoft AI technologies, skills, learning paths, and certification opportunities into a visual progression.

The goal is to make a complex technology ecosystem easier to understand by showing learners what they can learn next and how different skills connect.
